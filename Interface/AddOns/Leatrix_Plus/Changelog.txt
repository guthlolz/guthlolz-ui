- Added 'Show info cards' ('Interface').  If checked, pointing at something (such as a player, item, spell, buff, quest, etc) and pressing a keybind (you can set it in the configuration panel) will show a color-coded information card.  Pointing at a character will show information such as their average item level, talent choices, buffs and a 3D model of the character with animation slider, a gear toggle button, a fullscreen switch and scene selector.
